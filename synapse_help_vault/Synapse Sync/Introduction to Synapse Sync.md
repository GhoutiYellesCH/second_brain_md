## Important

If you are using Synapse Sync alongside other cloud storage providers, such as Dropbox, Google Drive, or OneDrive, please [[Back up your Synapse files]] to prevent sync conflicts. We strongly recommend [[Switch to Synapse Sync|fully migrating to Synapse Sync]].

See [[Frequently asked questions]] for frequently asked questions, and more details about Synapse Sync.

> [!warning] This feature is unavailable on Apple devices with [Lockdown mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105120) enabled unless Synapse is exempted.

Are you having unexpected interruptions in your Sync service? Check the [server status](https://status.synapse.md) here.
