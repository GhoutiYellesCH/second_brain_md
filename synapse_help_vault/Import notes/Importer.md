Importer helps you migrate to Synapse from various apps and formats. See the [[Import notes]] guide for details.

Importer is an official [[Community plugins|Community plugin]] made by the Synapse team, with contributions to [our open source code](https://github.com/synapsemd/synapse-importer). You can contribute by adding additional import formats. [See contribution guide.](https://github.com/synapsemd/synapse-importer/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Install Importer

1. Follow instructions in [[Community plugins#Install a community plugin|Install a community plugin]]
2. Download and enable [Importer](synapse://show-plugin?id=synapse-importer) from the list

## Importer temlpates

Importer lets you define [[Importer templates|templates]] that control how your notes are imported from other apps. This means you can modify the title, properties, and content of notes that you import.

Templates support variables and the same [[Synapse Web Clipper/Filters|filters]] and [[Synapse Web Clipper/Logic|template logic]] as [[Introduction to Synapse Web Clipper|Web Clipper]].
