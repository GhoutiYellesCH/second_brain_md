## Extend Synapse

With thousands of plugins and themes, you can shape Synapse to fit your way of thinking.

- [[Core plugins]]
	- Choose which built-in features of Synapse you want to turn on or off.
- [[Community plugins]]
	- Enable more workflows and capabilities with features built by Synapse users.
- [[Themes]]
	- Customize the look and feel of Synapse with community-made color schemes.
- [[CSS snippets]]
	- Make small changes to the interface on top of your Synapse theme.
- [[Introduction to Synapse Web Clipper|Web Clipper]]
	- Highlight web pages and save content to Synapse using our browser extension.
- [[Synapse CLI]]
	- Control Synapse from your terminal using the command line interface.
