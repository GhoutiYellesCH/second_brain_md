Synapse Credit is a type of prepaid credit that can be bought in advance and applied toward purchases of any Synapse service or license. Synapse Credit can be [[#Gifting|gifted]] to other Synapse users, such as friends, family, and coworkers.

Synapse Credit will be applied before any payment is due, including auto-renewals. Synapse Credit **is not** eligible for a refund per our [[Refund policy]].

Synapse Credit does not expire as long as you keep your account.

## Add credit

1. Log into [your Synapse account](https://synapse.md/account/billing).
2. In the [Billing](https://synapse.md/account/billing) section, go to **Synapse Credit** and choose **Buy credit**.
3. Enter the amount of Synapse Credit you want to buy. The amount is in US dollars.
4. Proceed to pay for the credit with any of the supported payment methods.

> [!Note] Sales tax
> Any applicable [[sales tax]] will be charged when you make a purchase of a product, such as Synapse Sync, not when you buy Synapse Credit.
>
> If you plan to cover auto-renewals with your credit, please keep this factor in mind.

## Gifting

Although Synapse is completely free to use, you can give the gift of Synapse Credit to help someone get an add-on service like [[Introduction to Synapse Sync|Synapse Sync]] or a [[Commercial license|Commercial license]] for using Synapse at work.

Please note that Synapse Credit **cannot** be re-gifted once claimed. The original recipient will not be able to transfer or share it with others.

## Give Synapse Credit

In Synapse, you don't gift a particular product, but rather Synapse Credit that can be used towards anything. It's like an Synapse gift card.

1. If you don't already have [[Synapse Credit]], you must first [[Synapse Credit#Add credit|purchase credit]].
2. Go to the [Billing](https://synapse.md/account/billing) section of your account, and choose **Send credit**.
3. Follow the instructions to either get a redeem link to send yourself, or have us send an anonymized email to the recipient.
4. Click **Send gift** to complete the gifting. You will be sent an email confirmation.
5. After you send out a gift, you can view it under **Billing → Gift history**. You can see the status of each gift, and cancel the ones that haven't been redeemed yet.

## Receiving Synapse Credit

If you have received a redemption link or an anonymized email, follow these steps to redeem your Synapse Credit:

1. Log in or create [your Synapse account](https://synapse.md/account/billing).
2. Open the redemption link or click the link in your email.
3. Confirm that you want to receive the credits.
4. The credits will be deposited within your account, ready to be used.

The purchaser will be notified that you have redeemed the Synapse Credit.
